# Quantifying the Impact of Translation Errors on Multilingual LLM Evaluation

ATR source ID: `SRC-TRANSLATION-ERRORS-2026`

## 来源支持的内容

It finds benchmark translation errors associated with measurable accuracy losses after controls, and tests the limits of LLM-produced translation-error spans against expert annotations.

## 来源不支持 / 不能推出的内容

It is not an agent-action evaluation study and does not establish a task/evaluator localization interaction.

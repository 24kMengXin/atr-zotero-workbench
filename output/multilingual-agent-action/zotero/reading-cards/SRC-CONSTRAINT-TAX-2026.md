# Constraint Tax in Open-Weight LLMs: An Empirical Study of Tool Calling Suppression Under Structured Output Constraints

ATR source ID: `SRC-CONSTRAINT-TAX-2026`

## 来源支持的内容

Joint tool-calling and JSON-schema constraints can suppress tool invocation in reported open-weight deployments; the paper treats this as an implementation/decoding interaction and proposes a two-pass mitigation.

## 来源不支持 / 不能推出的内容

It does not test multilingual language roles, semantic argument-value correctness, or a multilingual function-calling benchmark.

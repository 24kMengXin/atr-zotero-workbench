# MPR-GUI: Benchmarking and Enhancing Multilingual Perception and Reasoning in GUI Agents

ATR source ID: `SRC-MPRGUI-2026`

## 来源支持的内容

The authors identify lack of strictly aligned cross-lingual environments and fine-grained diagnostics as obstacles to attributing multilingual GUI-agent failures; their aligned evaluation finds reasoning-intensive gaps.

## 来源不支持 / 不能推出的内容

A GUI-specific aligned environment does not establish that token count explains other agent failures or that a formal-world diagnostic transports to natural languages.

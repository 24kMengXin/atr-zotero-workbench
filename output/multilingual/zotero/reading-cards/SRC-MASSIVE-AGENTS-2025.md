# MASSIVE-Agents: A Benchmark for Multilingual Function-Calling in 52 Languages

ATR source ID: `SRC-MASSIVE-AGENTS-2025`

## 来源支持的内容

The benchmark contains 47,020 samples over 52 languages, 55 functions and 286 arguments; it reports substantial AST-accuracy variation and includes prompt-language and latency ablations.

## 来源不支持 / 不能推出的内容

It does not identify a token-budget mechanism, separate all semantic realization effects from tokenization, or test representation-aware budget allocation.
